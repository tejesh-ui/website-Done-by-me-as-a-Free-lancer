Refund & Cancellation Policy

How cancellations, changes and refunds work for bookings and orders with ALLEN Kitchen & Bar.

Last updated: October 2026

Because our dishes are freshly prepared, perishable food items, we follow the policy below.

Dine-in and takeaway

Please check your order before you leave the outlet. As freshly prepared food, items generally cannot be returned. If there is a problem, tell a member of our team the same day.

Cancelling or changing a booking or order

  • Contact us as early as possible to change or cancel a reservation or order.
  • Once preparation of a custom, party or event order has started, it may not be possible to cancel it or refund the advance.
  • Any cancellation terms for large bookings will be shared when your booking is confirmed.

Quality concerns

If you're not satisfied with the quality of a dish or drink, please tell us at the outlet or contact us within 24 hours with your bill and, where possible, a photo. We will review the concern and, where appropriate, offer a replacement or refund.

Refunds

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method. The time taken to reflect in your account depends on your bank or payment provider.
