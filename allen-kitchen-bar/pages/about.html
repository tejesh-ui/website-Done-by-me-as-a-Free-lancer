Our story

ALLEN is a multi-cuisine kitchen and bar built around good food, handcrafted drinks and easy evenings.

Our menu brings North Indian, South Indian, Chinese and Continental plates together with cocktails, mocktails and desserts, so every table finds something to share. We value fresh ingredients, careful cooking and warm hospitality.
