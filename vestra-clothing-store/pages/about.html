Our story

VESTRA is a clothing store for men and women built around wardrobe essentials and standout pieces that are easy to wear.

From shirts, tees and pants to hoodies, blazers, dresses and kurtis, we choose pieces for their fit, fabric and finish — and we’re happy to help you find the right size and style.

Visit one of our stores or message us — see our stores page for addresses and directions.
