Returns & Exchange Policy

How exchanges, returns and refunds work for orders with VESTRA Clothing Store.

Last updated: October 2026

We want you to be happy with what you buy. Please read the policy below.

Exchanges

  • Unworn items with the original tags and your bill can be exchanged for another size or colour, subject to stock.
  • Please contact us or visit your store as soon as possible after your purchase — the exchange window for your order is confirmed when you buy.
  • Items that have been worn, washed or altered cannot be exchanged.

Items that cannot be exchanged

Innerwear, socks, items bought on final sale, and custom or bulk orders cannot normally be exchanged. This will be mentioned when you order.

Faulty or damaged items

If an item arrives faulty or damaged, tell us within 48 hours with your bill and, where possible, a photo. We will review the concern and, where appropriate, offer a replacement, exchange or refund.

Cancelling an order

Contact us as early as possible to cancel an order. Once preparation of a custom or bulk order has started, it may not be possible to cancel it or refund the advance.

Refunds

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method. The time taken to reflect in your account depends on your bank or payment provider.
