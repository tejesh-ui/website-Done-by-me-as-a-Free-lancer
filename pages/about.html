Community Ethos

Live Better. Live Together.

Green Haven Residences was conceived not merely as high-rise towers, but as an enduring ecosystem of wellness, natural harmony, and multi-generational connection.

Our Vision

A Complete Community Rather Than Just Buildings

In an era of dense urban isolation, Green Haven Residences returns to what truly enriches human life: expansive open grounds, clean unpolluted air, spaces for spontaneous conversations, and homes that nurture peace.

Over 80% of our master-planned acreage is dedicated to pristine landscaping, botanical gardens, and vehicle-free promenades, providing every resident with an immediate sanctuary from the city bustle.

Sustainable Engineering

Green by Design

Responsible ecological stewardship built directly into our infrastructure to ensure a lighter carbon footprint.

150+ Indigenous Flora

Locally adapted botanical and medicinal species that thrive with minimal water consumption and support native pollinator ecosystems.

100% Water Recycling

Advanced on-site sewage treatment plants and multi-stage rainwater percolation pits ensuring zero water discharge into city storm drains.

Rooftop photovoltaic arrays power all common community pathway lighting, paired with universal electric vehicle charging stations.
