Frequently Asked Questions

Site Visit Essentials

What happens during the site visit?

You will be greeted at our Welcome Pavilion, given a golf cart tour of the botanical parks and amenities, and led through furnished sample residences corresponding to your desired bedroom configuration.

Is parking available on-site for visitors?

Yes, complimentary valet parking is provided directly in front of the Experience Centre on Haven Boulevard.

Are exact prices shared during the tour?

Our senior advisors will present transparent unit availability schedules, customized payment plans, and floor cost breakdowns tailored to your chosen tower orientation.
